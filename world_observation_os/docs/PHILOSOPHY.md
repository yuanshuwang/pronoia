# Design Philosophy

## Core

- **Observe first. Interpret slowly.**
- **Record reality before imposing narratives.**

## The system helps you

- Browse high-signal content more efficiently
- Notice recurring emotional patterns
- See workaround behavior
- Track lifestyle shifts over time
- Reduce noise
- Preserve long-term observations
- Build worldview manually

## The system does NOT

- Generate startup ideas automatically
- Rank opportunities
- Produce business strategy
- Predict markets
- Pretend certainty about human emotion

## AI (future, optional)

May assist: tagging, summarization, reading efficiency, organization.

Must NOT: opportunity ranking, autonomous conclusions, fake precision.

## Patterns

`pain_patterns` are **never auto-generated**. They emerge when **you** decide something has recurred over weeks or months.

## Uncertainty

Modern exhaustion, identity, loneliness, and lifestyle change are hard to model.

This tool is humble. It is for long-term observation — not a machine that claims to understand the world.
